✓Data Analysis Plan
Reference plan for analyzing collected survey responses.

1. Data Cleaning

  • Remove duplicate submissions (same browser fingerprint + timestamp < 2 min)
  • Handle missing values: drop responses with > 30% unanswered required fields
  • Recode "N/A" ratings as missing (exclude from mean calculation)
  • Validate identity-conditional logic (students answering teacher-specific Qs flagged)

2. Descriptive Statistics

  • Demographics: Bar chart of identity types, pie chart of academic fields
  • Awareness: Stacked bar of discovery channels × identity
  • Frequency: Histogram of usage frequency by role group

3. Satisfaction Analysis

  • Module mean scores: Bar chart of avg score per feature (excl. N/A)
  • Heatmap: Feature × Role group mean satisfaction
  • NPS-style: Recommend score distribution (Promoters 9-10, Passives 7-8, Detractors 1-6)
  • Gap analysis: Identify features with highest variance (suggests inconsistent UX)

4. Comparative Analysis (Group Differences)

  • Student vs. Teacher: Independent t-test / Mann-Whitney U on overall satisfaction
  • By experience level: ANOVA across 5 experience groups for each module score
  • By academic field: Kruskal-Wallis test for field-based differences in feature priorities

5. Text Analysis (Open-ended)

  • Keyword extraction: TF-IDF to identify top themes in pain points and suggestions
  • Sentiment analysis: Classify feedback as positive / neutral / negative
  • Topic modeling: LDA to group suggestions into thematic clusters

6. Priority Matrix

  • Feature request count × avg satisfaction of requesters
  • Quadrant: High demand + Low satisfaction = Critical fix
  • Quadrant: High demand + High satisfaction = Expand & promote

7. Tools & Export

  • Export: Survey responses → JSON → pandas DataFrame → CSV
  • Visualization: matplotlib / seaborn / plotly for charts
  • Statistics: scipy.stats for hypothesis testing
  • NLP: jieba (Chinese tokenization) + scikit-learn TfidfVectorizer
